A-GNP-C Practice Exams 1–5 AANP format

Advanced NP Certification Review Services
by Dr. Adekemi Akinlawon, DNP, PMHNP-BC, APRN

Full-length mock exams built to the AANPCB Adult-Gerontology Primary Care NP (A-GNP) blueprint: Assess 28%, Diagnose 25%, Plan 25%, Evaluate 22%, with items spread across the AANPCB age groups (adolescent to elderly). Each mock has 175 questions — 157 scored and 18 unscored pretest items, mixed together as on the real exam (the live AANPCB exam has 150 items: 135 scored, 15 pretest).
